parrot

Don't type. Speak.

Open-source push-to-talk dictation for macOS. On-device, ultrafast. No account, no subscription, no cloud.

DownloadSource

macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon · free, MIT

Talk to any text field

Hold fn, speak, and let go. Your words appear wherever your cursor is, usually in under 300ms, in any app.

Everything happens on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing you say is saved.

Teach it your words: add the names and terms you use, and Parrot spells them your way.
